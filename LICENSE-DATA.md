# Data licence

This covers the register and catalogue text in this repository. The code is under the AGPL
([`LICENSE`](LICENSE)); the clients in `clients/` are under MIT.

## Our text: CC BY 4.0

The text National Digital writes for publicdata.au is licensed under the
[Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International licence](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/)
(CC BY 4.0). That is:

- the register entries in `register/` (titles, summaries, descriptions, FAQs, search titles,
  keywords, field labels, examples, charts, samples, topics and notes);
- the manifests in `store/` and the hub record `store/hubs.json`;
- the same text as it appears on publicdata.au and in its API.

Attribute it as: **publicdata.au, National Digital, licensed under CC BY 4.0**, with a link to
<https://publicdata.au> where the medium allows.

## Publishers' text and data: their licence

Some of the text in a register entry or manifest is the publisher's own: dataset titles and
descriptions, field descriptions, licence statements and attributions as the portal published
them. That text stays under the publisher's licence, which the same entry records, and CC BY 4.0
grants nothing over it.

The government datasets themselves are not in this repository, apart from the test extracts
listed in [`THIRD-PARTY-NOTICES.md`](THIRD-PARTY-NOTICES.md). Each dataset is republished under
its publisher's licence, recorded in its register entry and manifest and shown on its page with
the attribution that licence asks for. publicdata.au adds no rights of its own to a publisher's
data and takes none away.

## What is not licensed

The publicdata.au and National Digital names and marks: see [`BRAND.md`](BRAND.md).
